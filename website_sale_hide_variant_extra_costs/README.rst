===================================================
eCommerce: Hide Variant Extra Price on Product Page
===================================================

* Adds option to hide the texts such as '+ 15 €' from variant dropdowns


Configuration
=============
* Go to website settings and check the 'Hide Variant Costs from Product Page Dropdowns'
  checkbox. Note that the setting is company specific


Usage
=====
* Open product page in frontend after changing the setting, and the extra price texts
  no longer show as parts of the dropdowns. The total price below the variant selections 
  will still update as before.
