================================
eCommerce: Customer's Birth Year
================================

This module lets customers fill in their year of birth during website
checkout when their cart contains a membership product.

When the cart contains at least one product flagged as a membership
product, the checkout address step shows an extra Year of Birth field.
The value is saved on the customer's contact record. If the contact
already has a year of birth set, it is pre-filled.

=============
\-

Usage
=====

#. Add a membership product to the cart and proceed to checkout.
#. On the address step, fill in the Year of Birth field.
#. Submit the address - the value is saved on the customer's contact.
