===========================================
eCommerce: Address form improved guide text
===========================================

* Replaces the generic "Edit address" / "New address" heading of the checkout
  address form with one that attempts to give more info about which address 
  is being edited.
* Especially intended for newly invited portal users, who get taken straight to
  the address form on their first checkout without context of whether they
  are dealing with their own, billing or delivery address.

Configuration
=============
\-

Usage
=====
1. Log in, add items to the cart and proceed to checkout.
2. Open the address form, e.g. via "Add Address" or by editing an address.

Known issues / Roadmap
